**What the acceptance covers:**
1. **Domain rules, each with failure cases:**
   - Trimming to a natural return point is honest because every frame still comes from the source; an unrequested crossfade or reverse hides that the clip doesn't loop.
   - Low-saturation backdrops are never auto-keyed. A user-supplied `--key` on one must either keep the figure or refuse in plain language.
   - The default key removes all background-coloured areas, including enclosed ones, and must always warn about them with the `--keep-interior-key` option. The chibi frame-54 pocket and the C1 inner box are the paired examples. There is also a rule against the current always-false warning check (`and False`) in `apply_key_connected`.
   - Pixel art is checked by measurement: every s×s block is a single colour, colour count ≤ `--colors`, transparency is fully on or off, and no smooth resizing. The portrait idle video gives an odd MP4 height, which must be padded rather than resized.
2. **S-8 to S-12:** exact commands, expected values, exit codes and forbidden outcomes for each. Two helper scripts (`measure2.py`, `pixelcheck.py`) go in `/tmp`. S-11 uses robot, seated, idle and gufeng as unseen test videos, and the builder's verdict for each must match my rerun. Batch 1 S-1..S-7 must all still pass, and S-1's ≤ 0.02 magenta limit, which Batch 1 never met, is now required.
3. **Non-goal gates:** I recorded the sha256 of all three `examples/*/final.gif` files. There are diff checks on `.github/workflows` and `run_ci_smoke.py`, and a ban on Grok/fal calls, Agensi submission and X posting.
